Tap • Share • Grow

Exchange professional contact info instantly through NFC or QR codes. No more business cards or manual entry, just tap and connect.

Download APK Source Code App Repo Website Repo

Features

  • NFC Tap-to-Share: Hold phones back-to-back to exchange profiles instantly
  • QR Code: Generate and scan QR codes when NFC isn't available
  • Custom Profiles: Add your photo, bio, social links, and more
  • Connection Dashboard: View and manage all your connections in one place
  • Map View: See where you met each connection
  • Analytics: Track your networking stats over time
  • Cloud Sync: All data backed up securely to Firebase with built-in authentication
